# Old Idea - Vault Metrics

What if a vault's word count over time was a proxy for how much thinking you are actually doing? Not notes created, but words revised.

Most note apps reward capture. You clip, you save, you file, and the pile grows. But a pile is not understanding. The notes that matter are the ones you come back to and rewrite, because rewriting is where you find out what you actually think.

So resurfacing old notes might matter more than writing new ones. A display that shows one forgotten note a day would be a gentle way to do that.
