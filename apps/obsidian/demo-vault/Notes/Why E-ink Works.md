# Why E-ink Works

An e-ink screen only draws when something changes, and it holds the image without power. That makes it a strange kind of display: it is closer to paper pinned to a wall than to a monitor.

Because it refreshes slowly, nothing on it can demand your attention. There are no notifications, no animation and no glow. You look at it the way you look at a clock, when you want to, and then you look away.

That is exactly what a personal dashboard should be. The best things to put on one are facts that change a few times a day: the weather, the next train, the three tasks that matter today.
