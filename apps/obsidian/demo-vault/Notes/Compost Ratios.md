# Compost Ratios

Roughly two parts brown to one part green by volume. Browns are dry leaves, cardboard and straw; greens are kitchen scraps and grass clippings.

If the heap smells, add browns. If nothing is happening, add greens and water. Turn it every couple of weeks in summer and leave it alone over winter. A heap that is too small never gets warm, so aim for at least a cubic metre before judging it.
