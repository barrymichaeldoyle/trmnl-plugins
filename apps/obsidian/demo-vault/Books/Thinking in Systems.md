# Thinking in Systems

## Chapter 1: The Basics
A system is a set of things interconnected in such a way that they produce their own pattern of behaviour over time. The behaviour comes from the structure, not from outside events.

## Chapter 2: A Brief Visit to the Systems Zoo
Stocks change only through flows. A bathtub fills or drains at the rate of the difference between the tap and the plughole, which is why stocks act as buffers and why they take time to change.
